# 🧾 Insurance & Hospital Bills for Foreign Visitors

<div class="plain-summary">
  <strong class="plain-summary-label">Plain English</strong>
  <p>Many hospitals want payment first. International departments cost more but reduce language friction. Keep every receipt for insurance claims.</p>
  <p>Call your insurer before expensive care when you can safely wait.</p>

**TL;DR:** Know whether your policy is **direct bill** or **pay-and-claim**. Bring passport + policy number + emergency assist phone. Photograph **every** bill and diagnosis note.

**Prerequisites:**
- Travel/expat medical policy PDF offline
- 24/7 assistance number saved as contact
- Payment capacity: card + Alipay + cash buffer ([Money Runtime](../../01-System-Setup/money-runtime/))
- [Hospital access](../hospital-access/) for how to enter care

---

## Problem

Visitors assume “insurance card = walk out free.” In China, **self-pay then claim** is common outside a few international clinics. Surprise bills and missing paperwork kill reimbursements.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Before you need care (pre-trip)

1. Confirm coverage includes **mainland China** and your activity type.
2. Note: deductible, max, COVID/sports exclusions, outpatient vs inpatient.
3. Save **policy number**, member ID, assist phone, email for claims.
4. If you have chronic meds, check [Prescription refill](../prescription-refill/) + medication legality.

### 2. Choose facility type

| Facility | Pros | Cons |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Public ER (三甲)** | Everywhere; 120 routes here | Crowds; language; process heavy |
| **International dept / clinic** | English more common; itemized bills | Higher cash prices |
| **Insurer network hospital** | Possible direct billing | Must call assist to confirm **today** |

**Action:** For non-ambulance cases, call **insurer assist first** if time allows — ask “cashless / direct bill available at [hospital]?”

### 3. On arrival at hospital

1. Passport + payment method ready.
2. Say you have **international insurance**; show card/PDF — do not assume automatic coverage.
3. Ask for **itemized invoice / 明细** and diagnosis papers.
4. For work claims, request **fapiao** where possible ([Money Runtime](../../01-System-Setup/money-runtime/)).

### 4. Documents to capture (minimum claim pack)

- [ ] Passport bio page  
- [ ] Policy number screenshot  
- [ ] Admission / outpatient record  
- [ ] Diagnosis (诊断)  
- [ ] Itemized bill (费用明细)  
- [ ] Payment proof (card slip / Alipay record)  
- [ ] Fapiao if issued  
- [ ] Prescription list  

Photograph paper forms before they leave your hands.

### 5. After payment

1. Notify insurer within policy window (often 24–48h for major cases).
2. Upload claim via app/email as required.
3. Keep originals until claim closes.
4. **Verification:** Claim reference number issued.

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

| Situation | Action |
|:---|:---|
| Hospital demands full prepay | Pay to treat first if emergency; claim later — life > paperwork |
| No English | Translation app + [phrase tools](../../01-System-Setup/translation-tools/); companion if available |
| Card declines large bill | Second card; wire options via insurer assist; embassy list is last resort not ATM |
| Insurer denies cashless | Self-pay path; insist on complete paperwork |
| Ambulance 120 | Goes to nearest appropriate public hospital — not always your preferred international clinic |
| Dispute amount | Written itemization; insurer medical team; do not sign blank forms |

---

## Cost shock control (order-of-magnitude mindset)

Prices vary wildly by city and facility. Mental model only:

- Simple outpatient consult + basic tests: often manageable on a card but not “EU public health free.”
- ER imaging + night stay: can jump to amounts that max a travel card — call issuer / insurer early.
- Always ask **estimate (大概多少钱)** before non-urgent add-on tests.

This is **not** a price list — verify on site.

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- Put assist number in phone as **ICE-INSURANCE**.
- International clinics often want **passport + credit card imprint** even with insurance letters.
- Kids: carry allergy card ([generator tool](../../../phrase-card-tool.html)) into ER.
- If flying soon after care, ask doctor for a **fit-to-fly** note when relevant.
