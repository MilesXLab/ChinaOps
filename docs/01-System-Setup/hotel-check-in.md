# 🏨 Hotel Check-in & Registration SOP

<div class="plain-summary">
  <strong class="plain-summary-label">Plain English</strong>
  <p>Hotels must register foreign guests with the police. Bring your passport and expect a scan or photo of it.</p>
  <p>If the front desk says they cannot accept foreigners, ask for another hotel or call your booking platform support.</p>
  <p><span class="scope-badge">Scope: national</span></p>
</div>

**TL;DR:** All hotels in China require passport verification and **24-hour registration** with local police. International chain hotels handle this automatically; smaller hotels may need manual assistance. Always carry your **physical passport** (digital copies are not accepted).

**Prerequisites:**
- **Physical Passport:** Original document (mandatory, no exceptions).
- **Booking Confirmation:** Digital or paper confirmation with hotel address in Chinese.
- **Payment Method:** Credit card or Alipay/WeChat Pay balance.
- **Chinese Address:** Hotel address written in Chinese characters (for registration form).

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Pre-Check-in Preparation
- **Step 1:** Ensure your passport has at least **6 months validity** from your check-in date.
- **Step 2:** Have your booking confirmation ready (digital is fine, but screenshot it for offline access).
- **Step 3:** Prepare the hotel's address in **Chinese characters** (ask the hotel or use Google Translate).
- **Verification:** Confirm you have all three items before arriving at the hotel.

### 2. Check-in Process (International Hotels)
- **Step 1:** Present your passport at the front desk.
- **Step 2:** The hotel staff will:
  - Scan your passport
  - Complete the **"Temporary Residence Registration"** (临时住宿登记) automatically
  - Submit it to the local police station electronically
- **Verification:** You should receive a confirmation (verbal or printed) that registration is complete.

### 3. Check-in Process (Smaller/Local Hotels)
- **Step 1:** Present your passport at the front desk.
- **Step 2:** If the hotel cannot complete registration automatically, they may ask you to:
  - Fill out a paper form manually
  - Visit the local police station yourself (rare, but possible)
- **Step 3:** If asked to visit police station, request the hotel provide:
  - Exact address of the police station (派出所)
  - A note in Chinese explaining your situation
- **Verification:** Ensure you receive a registration receipt before leaving the hotel.

---

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

### If the hotel rejects your passport or booking:
1. **The "International Chain" Failover:** If a small hotel cannot process your registration, move to an international chain hotel (Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, etc.). They have standardized systems and English-speaking staff.
2. **Booking Platform Support:** Contact your booking platform (Booking.com, Agoda, Trip.com) customer service. They can help resolve registration issues or find alternative accommodation.
3. **Police Station Direct:** If registration fails at the hotel, go to the nearest police station (派出所) with your passport and hotel confirmation. They can complete the registration manually (takes 15-30 minutes).

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- **The "24-Hour Rule":** You must register within **24 hours** of arrival. If you check in late at night, registration can be completed the next morning, but don't delay beyond 24 hours.
- **Digital Passport Copies:** Hotels will **not accept** digital copies or photos of your passport. Always carry the physical document.
- **Group Bookings:** If traveling with family, each person (including children) needs individual registration. Hotels handle this automatically, but be prepared for a longer check-in process.
- **The "Pre-Registration" Hack:** Some international hotels allow you to complete registration online before arrival. Check your booking confirmation email for a registration link.

## 🔄 Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue 1: Passport Verification Fails
- **Symptom:** Hotel scanner cannot read your passport chip or barcode.
- **Solution:** Ask staff to manually enter passport details. If that fails, request to visit the police station for manual registration.

### Issue 2: Booking Not Found
- **Symptom:** Hotel claims they don't have your reservation.
- **Solution:** Show your booking confirmation (email/app). If still unresolved, contact your booking platform's customer service (most have China-specific hotlines).

### Issue 3: Payment Method Rejected
- **Symptom:** Hotel cannot process your credit card or payment app.
- **Solution:** Use Alipay/WeChat Pay balance, or withdraw cash from a nearby ATM. Most hotels accept cash for deposits.

### Issue 4: Language Barrier
- **Symptom:** Hotel staff cannot speak English.
- **Solution:** Use translation apps (Google Translate, Pleco) or show this phrase: "Wǒ yào bànlǐ zhùsù dēngjì" (I need to complete accommodation registration).

---

## 🚩 Strategic Gap: The "SPOF" Warning
**Single Point of Failure:** Relying on a single hotel booking without backup options.
- **Hotfix:** Always have a backup hotel option (especially an international chain) in case your primary booking fails. Keep booking platform customer service numbers saved offline.
