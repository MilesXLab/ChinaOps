**Prerequisites:** National setup done: [Landing Protocol](../../01-System-Setup/landing-protocol/) · [Money Runtime](../../01-System-Setup/money-runtime/) · [Public transport](../public-transport-tips/)

## Problem

Shanghai-first habits (short metro hops, Bund mental map) fail in Beijing’s scale. Wrong airport transfer and winter air quality are the usual surprises.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Airports

| Code | Name (concept) | Note |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **PEK** | Capital | Often closer mental model to “classic Beijing” |
| **PKX** | Daxing | Long transfer to many hotels — budget 90–120+ min door-to-door |

**Action:** Confirm which airport is on your ticket **before** booking hotel shuttles. Use official taxi queue or DiDi — same black-cab rule as Shanghai.

### First-night corridor (Beijing)
- **Airports:** Confirm **PEK vs PKX** on the ticket before booking a hotel transfer.
- **Default first night:** CBD / Guomao / Sanlitun-class business hotels (passport desks used to foreigners).
- **Move:** Official taxi queue or DiDi from the correct airport — budget **60–120+ min** door-to-door from PKX.

### 2. Metro & pay

- Alipay / WeChat transit QR works on Beijing Subway in normal tourist flows (confirm in-app city).
- Expect **longer walks** between lines; follow 换乘 signs early.
- Peak hours are hard with luggage — allow slack.

**Verification:** Generate transit QR while on airport Wi‑Fi once.

### 3. Hotels & registration

Same national passport registration as [Hotel check-in](../../01-System-Setup/hotel-check-in/). In hutong / small guesthouses, re-confirm **外宾** acceptance — refusal risk similar to other heritage districts.

### 4. Embassies & documents

Beijing has the densest consular footprint. If you need visa runs, lost-passport work, or legalisation:

1. Star your embassy on maps **day one**.
2. Check appointment systems — walk-ins may not exist.
3. Carry paper copies; see [Lost passport](../../03-Emergency-DR/lost-passport/).

### 5. Weather / AQI delta

- Winters: dry cold + heating indoors.
- AQI spikes: mask in bag; see weather habits from [Shanghai weather guide](../shanghai-weather-guide/) mental model (check local AQI apps/widgets).

### 6. First 24h checklist (Beijing)

- [ ] Airport identity correct on tickets  
- [ ] Hotel Chinese address + metro line known  
- [ ] Transit QR tested  
- [ ] Embassy pinned  
- [ ] Cash ¥500+ ([Money Runtime](../../01-System-Setup/money-runtime/))

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

| Issue | Move |
|:---|:---|
| Landed wrong airport assumption | Recalculate DiDi time/cost; do not “cheap black car” |
| Metro QR fails | Staff ticket machines with passport where supported; or taxi |
| Small hutong hotel refuses | International brand inside 3rd Ring as bridge |
| Bad AQI day | Indoor museums / malls; N95 for sensitive travelers |
| Need urgent consular help | Embassy duty phone from [Emergency contacts](../../03-Emergency-DR/emergency-contacts-card/) pattern — verify current number |
