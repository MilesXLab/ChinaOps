🚨 Pre-flight "Zero-Downtime" Checklist

Run these health checks BEFORE boarding your flight. Next major holiday: Mid-Autumn Festival (Sep 25–27) and National Day Golden Week (Oct 1–7) — book HSR now:

  • ✅ Power Redundancy: Is your Power Bank in carry-on? (Phone is your life-line).
  • ✅ VPN Handshake: Logged into VPN apps at home to ensure they are updated?
  • ✅ Payment Setup: Cards bound to Alipay? (Auth "China" transactions with bank).
  • ✅ DR Strategy: Search and "Favorite" an International Hospital in your map app.
  • ✅ Translation Fix: Screenshot of your hotel’s name/address in Hanzi (Characters).
  • ✅ Offline Cache: Download Pleco dictionary and English offline maps for Shanghai/Beijing.

🔍 Why Open Source?

ChinaOps is open-source because we believe navigating a country shouldn't be a "black box" operation.

🛡️ Transparency

Every step in our SOPs is open for inspection. No hidden affiliate links, no paid rankings—just what actually works.

🏗️ Community Fixes

Travel policies and apps change fast. Open source allows the community to submit "hot-patches" so the guides stay accurate.

🆓 Zero Anxiety

We don't manufacture anxiety to sell "secrets." Experimental or survival info should be free and accessible to all families.

🚀 Coming in v2.0

The Next Evolution: We are already planning the next iteration of the ChinaOps framework, moving from "Survival" to "Optimization".

What should be the first "feature" of Version 2.0? We want to build what you actually need on the ground. No guesses, just real-world solutions.
