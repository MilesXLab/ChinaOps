Rules applied

  • Uppercase ASCII (accents stripped, e.g. José → JOSE)
  • Hyphens, apostrophes, and spaces removed (Jean Luc → JEANLUC)
  • Format: SURNAME<<GIVENNAMES (same as the train-ticket SOP)
  • If 12306 has separate Surname / Given fields, fill those from the passport instead of pasting <<
  • Verify in 12306 ~24h before travel; bring passport to the station
