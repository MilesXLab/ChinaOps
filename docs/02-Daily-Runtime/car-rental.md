# 🚗 Car Rental & Driving in China SOP

**TL;DR:** International driving permits are **not recognized** in China. You need a **Chinese driver's license** (obtained through a test) or a **temporary license** (valid 3 months, requires home license + translation). Most travelers use **chauffeur services** or **rental cars with drivers** instead of self-driving.

**Prerequisites:**
- **Home Driver's License:** Valid license from your home country.
- **International Driving Permit (IDP):** If you have one (note: not valid in China, but useful for translation).
- **Passport:** Original document.
- **Translation:** Certified translation of your home license (can be done in China).

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Understanding License Requirements
- **Step 1:** China **does not recognize** International Driving Permits (IDP) for driving.
- **Step 2:** Options for driving in China:
  - **Option A:** Obtain a Chinese driver's license (requires written test + road test, takes 1-2 weeks)
  - **Option B:** Get a temporary license (valid 3 months, requires translation of home license)
  - **Option C:** Use chauffeur/rental services with drivers (recommended for most travelers)
- **Verification:** Confirm which option applies to your situation before booking a rental car.

### 2. Temporary License (3-Month Option)
- **Step 1:** Get your home license **translated** into Chinese:
  - Location: Local **Traffic Management Bureau** (交通管理局) or authorized translation services
  - Cost: 50-200 RMB
  - Time: 1-3 days
- **Step 2:** Apply for temporary license at Traffic Management Bureau:
  - Required documents:
    - Original passport
    - Original home driver's license
    - Certified translation of license
    - Health certificate (can be obtained at local hospital, 50-100 RMB)
    - 2 passport photos
- **Step 3:** Take a **written test** (in English, multiple choice, 100 questions, need 90% to pass).
- **Verification:** Temporary license is valid for 3 months and can be renewed once.

### 3. Rental Car Options
- **Option A: Self-Drive Rental**
  - **Companies:** Hertz, Avis, Budget (international chains)
  - **Requirements:** Chinese license or temporary license
  - **Cost:** 300-800 RMB/day (depending on car type)
  - **Insurance:** Mandatory, usually included
- **Option B: Rental with Driver (Recommended)**
  - **Companies:** Local services, hotel concierge, or apps like **Didi Chauffeur**
  - **Cost:** 500-1,500 RMB/day (depending on car type and distance)
  - **Advantages:** No license needed, driver knows routes, avoids traffic violations

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

### If you cannot obtain a license or rental car:
1. **The "Chauffeur Service" Node:** Use **Didi Chauffeur** (滴滴专车) or hotel concierge services. More expensive but hassle-free.
2. **Public Transport:** China's high-speed rail and metro systems are extensive and often faster than driving in cities.
3. **Tour Companies:** For intercity travel, consider tour companies that provide transportation (often includes driver and guide).

---

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- **The "Don't Drive" Recommendation:** Unless you're staying long-term (6+ months), **don't drive in China**. Traffic rules are different, road signs are in Chinese, and insurance claims can be complicated for foreigners.
- **Traffic Rules Differences:**
  - **Right-hand drive:** China drives on the right (like US/Europe)
  - **Speed limits:** Strictly enforced, cameras everywhere
  - **Parking:** Complex rules, easy to get tickets
  - **Tolls:** Many highways have tolls (cash or ETC card)
- **The "Chauffeur" Hack:** For day trips or airport transfers, hiring a driver is often cheaper and less stressful than renting a car. Many hotels can arrange this.
- **Insurance is Critical:** If you do drive, ensure you have comprehensive insurance. Accidents involving foreigners can be complicated legally.

## 🔄 Alternative Transportation (Recommended)

### For City Travel
- **Metro/Subway:** Extensive networks in Tier-1 cities (Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou)
- **Taxis/Didi:** Convenient and affordable
- **Bike Sharing:** HelloBike, Meituan Bikes (for short distances)

### For Intercity Travel
- **High-Speed Rail (HSR):** Fast, comfortable, affordable (12306 booking)
- **Domestic Flights:** Competitive prices, extensive network
- **Long-Distance Buses:** Cheapest option, but less comfortable

### For Day Trips
- **Tour Companies:** Provide transportation + guide
- **Chauffeur Services:** Flexible, can customize itinerary
- **Public Transport:** Metro + bus combinations

---

## 🚩 Strategic Gap: The "SPOF" Warning
**Single Point of Failure:** Relying on self-driving without understanding license requirements or traffic rules.
- **Hotfix:** For most travelers, use public transport, taxis, or chauffeur services. Only consider self-driving if staying long-term and willing to obtain a Chinese license. Always have backup transportation plans.
