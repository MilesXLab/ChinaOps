## Problem

People under-plan West Lake crowds and over-plan “it’s just like Shanghai.” Station-to-lake transfers and rain change the day.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. From Shanghai
- HSR is the default; verify name format on 12306 ([MRZ tool](../../../mrz-tool.html)).
- Arrive **East / other stations** — confirm which station your ticket uses before booking hotel.

### First-night corridor (Hangzhou)
- **From Shanghai:** HSR to the station printed on your ticket (often East or other — verify); metro/DiDi to hotel.
- **Default first night:** Near metro **1–2 stops from West Lake** if lake hotels are sold out or expensive.
- **Day trip mode:** If returning to Shanghai same night, hold a **late return seat** before you leave the station area.

### 2. Local move
- Metro + DiDi for lake edges; walking density high on scenic paths.
- Bikes common; need working Alipay deposit flow ([App stack](../../01-System-Setup/china-app-stack/)).

### 3. Tourism delta
- West Lake free perimeter paths still jam at sunset/weekends.
- Museums / themed parks: tickets and ID rules — passport ready.
- Tea-tourist traps exist — fixed-price menus and walk-away skill apply ([Scams](../../03-Emergency-DR/safety-and-common-scams/)).

### 4. Weather
- Humid summers; umbrella season is real.
- AQI usually milder than north China winters, but check on bad regional days.

### 5. Hotels
- Lake-view premium spikes on holidays — book early or stay near metro 1–2 stops out.
- Confirm foreigner registration for boutique properties.

### 6. First 24h checklist
- [ ] Correct Hangzhou station on ticket  
- [ ] Hotel vs lake transfer time known  
- [ ] Transit QR works  
- [ ] Rain layer packed  
- [ ] Cash + QR both OK

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

| Issue | Move |
|:---|:---|
| Lake overcrowded | Museums, quieter parks, malls; shift to early morning next day |
| HSR sold out back to SH | Later trains; overnight stay; avoid random drivers |
| Hotel full near lake | Business hotels near metro; [Stay beyond hotel](../../01-System-Setup/stay-beyond-hotel/) |
| Heavy rain | Indoor food streets / malls; delay boat plans |
| Payment glitch | Second app + cash ([Money Runtime](../../01-System-Setup/money-runtime/)) |

---

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- Day trip: last trains fill on Sunday nights — hold a backup seat.
- Families: stroller on lake paths is mixed; soft carrier helps.
- Combine with Shanghai food/vegan guides for restaurant standards; local chains differ.
