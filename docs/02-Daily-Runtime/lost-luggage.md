# 🧳 Lost or Delayed Luggage Recovery SOP

<div class="plain-summary">
  <strong class="plain-summary-label">Plain English</strong>
  <p>Report delayed bags at the airline desk before you leave the airport. Keep the PIR reference number.</p>
  <p>Photograph essentials you still carry; ask about delivery to your hotel.</p>
  <p><span class="scope-badge">Scope: national</span></p>
</div>

**TL;DR:** Report missing luggage immediately at the airport **before leaving**. Get a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) with a reference number. Airlines typically deliver delayed luggage within 24-48 hours. Keep receipts for essential purchases (toiletry, clothing) for reimbursement claims.

**Prerequisites:**
- **Baggage Claim Tags:** Keep your baggage claim stubs (usually attached to your boarding pass).
- **Flight Details:** Booking reference, flight number, and arrival time.
- **Hotel Address:** Your accommodation address in Chinese characters (for delivery).
- **Essential Items List:** Mental note of critical items in checked luggage (medications, documents).

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Immediate Report (At Airport)
- **Step 1:** Do **not** leave the baggage claim area until you've reported missing luggage.
- **Step 2:** Go to the **Baggage Service Office** (行李服务处) or **Lost & Found** counter (usually near baggage claim).
- **Step 3:** Present your:
  - Boarding pass
  - Baggage claim tag
  - Passport
- **Step 4:** Fill out a **Property Irregularity Report (PIR)** form. Ensure you receive:
  - A reference number (非常重要 - very important)
  - A copy of the report
  - Contact phone number for updates
- **Verification:** Confirm the airline has your correct hotel address and phone number.

### 2. Essential Items Purchase (Temporary Supplies)
- **Step 1:** If your luggage contains critical items (medications, baby formula, documents), purchase replacements immediately.
- **Step 2:** Keep **all receipts** (fàpiào) for reimbursement claims.
- **Step 3:** Common airport purchases:
  - Toiletries: FamilyMart, Lawson (in airport)
  - Clothing: Uniqlo, H&M (if available in terminal)
  - Medications: Airport pharmacy (limited selection)
- **Verification:** Save receipts and take photos of them.

### 3. Follow-up Protocol
- **Step 1:** Call the airline's baggage service hotline daily (use the reference number):
  - **China Eastern:** +86-95530
  - **Air China:** +86-95583
  - **China Southern:** +86-95539
- **Step 2:** Check the airline's website with your reference number for status updates.
- **Verification:** Most airlines update status online within 24 hours.

---

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

### If luggage is not found after 48 hours:
1. **The "Insurance Claim" Node:** If you have travel insurance, file a claim for lost luggage. Most policies cover up to $1,000-3,000 for lost items.
2. **Embassy Assistance:** If critical documents (passport, visa) were in lost luggage, contact your embassy immediately for replacement procedures.
3. **Airline Compensation:** Airlines are required to compensate for lost luggage (usually up to 1,131 SDR ≈ $1,500 USD under Montreal Convention). File a formal claim with receipts.

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- **The "Carry-On Critical" Rule:** Never check critical items: medications, documents, electronics, or one change of clothes. Always keep these in carry-on.
- **Baggage Tag Photos:** Take a photo of your baggage claim tag immediately after checking in. If you lose the physical tag, the photo helps locate your bag.
- **The "AirTag" Hack:** Place an Apple AirTag or Tile tracker in checked luggage. This allows you to see the bag's location even if the airline's tracking fails.
- **Language Barrier:** If airport staff don't speak English, use translation apps or show this phrase: "Wǒ de xínglǐ diūle" (My luggage is lost). They will guide you to the right counter.

## 🔄 Recovery Protocol (When Luggage is Found)

### 1. Delivery Arrangement
- **Step 1:** When the airline locates your luggage, they will call you to confirm delivery address.
- **Step 2:** Provide your hotel address in **Chinese characters** and your room number.
- **Step 3:** Most airlines deliver within 24 hours of location, but confirm the delivery window.
- **Verification:** Request a delivery confirmation SMS or email.

### 2. Inspection & Damage Report
- **Step 1:** When luggage arrives, inspect it immediately for damage or missing items.
- **Step 2:** If items are missing or damaged, take photos and file a damage report with the airline.
- **Step 3:** Keep the delivery receipt and any damage reports for insurance claims.

### 3. Reimbursement Claim
- **Step 1:** Submit receipts for essential purchases (toiletry, clothing) to the airline.
- **Step 2:** Most airlines reimburse "reasonable" expenses (usually $50-200 USD) for delayed luggage.
- **Verification:** Follow up on reimbursement status (may take 2-4 weeks).

---

## 🚩 Strategic Gap: The "SPOF" Warning
**Single Point of Failure:** Checking all essential items (medications, documents, electronics) in luggage.
- **Hotfix:** Always maintain a "Go-Bag" in carry-on with 24-48 hours of essentials: medications, one change of clothes, charging cables, and critical documents.
