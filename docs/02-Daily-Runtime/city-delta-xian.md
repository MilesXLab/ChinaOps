## Problem

Day-trippers under-plan transfer time to the Terracotta site and over-trust friendly strangers near the Bell Tower / Muslim Quarter edges.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Gateways
- Airport and North rail station can be long rides from the wall — match hotel to tomorrow’s plan.
- Official taxi / DiDi only.

### 2. Metro & pay
- Transit QR via Alipay/WeChat (correct city).
- Far heritage sites often need metro + bus / official shuttle — budget time.

### 3. Tourism peaks
- Museums and wall gates sell out or queue hard on holidays.
- Night markets: watch bag; agree prices or use menus with prices.

### 4. Climate
- Drier winters; dusty wind days possible — lip balm / layers.
- Summers hot; water + hat on wall walks.

### 5. Hotels
- Confirm 外宾 for small guesthouses inside historic areas ([Stay beyond hotel](../../01-System-Setup/stay-beyond-hotel/)).

### 6. First 24h checklist
- [ ] Hotel Chinese address  
- [ ] Transit QR tested  
- [ ] Big-ticket booking held  
- [ ] Cash ¥500+  
- [ ] Scam walk-away phrases ready

| Issue | Move |
