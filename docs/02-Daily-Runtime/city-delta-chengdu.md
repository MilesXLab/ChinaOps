## Problem

Shanghai timing habits undershoot Chengdu tourism queues and overestimate “mild” Sichuan food. Foreigner-ready hotels cluster in certain districts.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Gateways
- Airport (CTU area) and high-speed rail stations can be far from your hotel — match district to first activity.
- Official taxi / DiDi only; same black-cab rule as other cities.

### 2. Metro & pay
- Transit QR via Alipay/WeChat (enable correct city).
- Core tourist lines get crowded at weekends — leave luggage buffer.

### 3. Food delta
- Default heat is **higher** than Shanghai benbang.
- Phrases: **不要太辣** / **微辣** / **不辣**.
- Peanut oil and numbing peppercorn show up often — allergy cards matter ([phrase tool](../../../phrase-card-tool.html)).

### 4. Tourism peaks
- Panda bases and holiday weeks: tickets sell out — book official channels early (see [Holiday survival](../../05-Event-Operations/holiday-survival-guide/) mindset).
- Hotpot evenings: cashless is fine; still keep small cash buffer.

### 5. Hotels
- Confirm **可接待外宾** for small boutique/homestay ([Stay beyond hotel](../../01-System-Setup/stay-beyond-hotel/)).
- CBD / Chunxi / wide business hotels usually scan passports smoothly.

### 6. First 24h checklist
- [ ] Hotel Chinese address  
- [ ] Transit QR tested  
- [ ] Spice / allergy card ready  
- [ ] Cash ¥500+  
- [ ] Next-day tickets already held

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

| Issue | Move |
|:---|:---|
| Panda tickets gone | Museum / park indoor day; do not use sketchy scalpers |
| Spice reaction | Stop eating; antihistamine if you carry; pharmacy 药店 |
| Hotel refuses foreigners | International or large Chinese chain in core |
| Rain all day | Malls + metro; delay day trips |
| Language at small eateries | Translate camera + point; or chain restaurants |
