## Problem

Shanghai flat-grid intuition fails on hills, bridges, and multi-level malls. People miss metro exits and climb unnecessary stairs with luggage.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Gateways
- Airport / rail to core can cross bridges and long metro rides — don’t schedule tight meetings after landing.
- DiDi pickup points can be multi-level; follow app pin + floor signs.

### 2. Metro & monorail
- Alipay/WeChat transit QR (city enabled).
- Note **exit numbers** — wrong exit can mean a huge climb.
- Luggage: elevators exist but are crowded; leave slack.

### 3. Navigation delta
- “Nearby” on the map may be across a cliff or river loop.
- Screenshot walking routes while online; offline maps help ([App stack](../../01-System-Setup/china-app-stack/)).

### 4. Food delta
- Hotpot and chuanchuan default spicy/numbing.
- Phrases: **微辣 / 不辣 / 不要香菜** as needed.
- Peanut and oil risks — use [phrase-card tool](../../../phrase-card-tool.html).

### 5. Climate
- Hot humid summers; foggy/drizzly stretches.
- Hydration + indoor AC breaks.

### 6. First 24h checklist
- [ ] Hotel pin + **exit number** known  
- [ ] Transit QR works  
- [ ] Luggage plan (elevator)  
- [ ] Spice / allergy card  
- [ ] Cash + QR

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

| Issue | Move |
|:---|:---|
| Lost between levels | Return to last metro station hall; ask staff with map screenshot |
| Walk “shortcut” fails | DiDi even for short hops with luggage |
| Too spicy / allergy | Stop; pharmacy; hospital if severe |
| Hotel access hard with bags | Hotel pickup / DiDi to door |
| Storm / heat warning | Indoor day; delay river/cable plans |
