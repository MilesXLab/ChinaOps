# 🏠 Stay Beyond Hotel: Short-term Rental & Rejected Check-in

<div class="plain-summary">
  <strong class="plain-summary-label">Plain English</strong>
  <p>Hotels must register foreign guests with police. Some small hotels refuse foreigners. Apartments and homestays need the same registration idea — just messier.</p>
  <p>Always have a Plan B international chain and your passport in hand.</p>
  <p><span class="scope-badge">Scope: national</span></p>
</div>

**TL;DR:** Confirm **外宾 (foreign guest)** acceptance **before** arrival. Passport must be scanned for **temporary residence registration** within **24 hours**. If refused, pivot to international chains or platform rebooking — do not sleep unregistered.

**Prerequisites:**
- Physical passport
- Booking with Chinese address
- Platform chat screenshots (Trip.com / Booking / Meituan / Airbnb-class apps)
- Backup hotel shortlist near your first-night area
- [Hotel check-in SOP](../hotel-check-in/) for standard front-desk flow

---

## Problem

“Hotel full” sometimes means **“we cannot process foreigners.”** Long-stay and homestay hosts may not know how to register you. Unregistered stays create police risk for you and the host.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Before you book (filter hard)

1. Message host/hotel: **可以接待外宾吗？需要护照登记。**
2. Prefer listings that explicitly say **foreigners OK / 可接待外宾**.
3. For first night after a long flight: default to **international brand** (Hilton / Marriott / IHG / Hyatt / Accor class) even if pricier.
4. Save **two backups** within 3 km with free cancellation if possible.

### 2. Standard hotel (recap)

Follow [Hotel check-in](../hotel-check-in/): passport scan → temporary residence registration → keep confirmation. Digital passport photos are **not** enough.

### 3. Homestay / short-term apartment

1. On arrival, ask **who registers you with police** (host vs you vs property manager).
2. Typical good path: host takes you to **派出所 (local police station)** or uses a licensed property system with your passport.
3. Provide: passport bio page, entry stamp/visa page, phone number, address of stay.
4. **Verification:** You receive a registration receipt / host confirms system success; you can stay without being asked to “just hide.”

> [!WARNING]
> If a host says “no need to register, it’s fine” — treat as **red flag**. Either fix registration same day or leave.

### 4. Longer rentals (weeks–months) — field outline

Not full legal advice; practical order only:

1. Use reputable agents or branded apartments used to expats.
2. Contract in writing; note deposit (often large), payment schedule, inventory list with photos.
3. Confirm **who does police registration** and **Wi‑Fi real-name** setup.
4. Gate/fob access: get two keys/cards if possible.
5. **Verification:** You can enter building after 22:00 without host present; registration done within 24h of move-in.

### 5. Night-of refusal protocol

1. Stay calm; ask reason: system? license? full?
2. Call **booking platform** support (in-app) for free rebooking / refund.
3. Open map → filter **international hotels** → book refundable if possible.
4. Official taxi / DiDi to new address only.
5. **Verification:** New booking confirmation + successful passport registration at new desk.

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

| Situation | Action |
|:---|:---|
| Small hotel refuses foreigners | International chain within city core; platform rebooking |
| Homestay cannot register | Move same day to licensed hotel |
| Arrive 01:00, everything closed | Airport hotel / 24h business hotel brands near rail/air hubs |
| Host demands cash-only no ID | Walk away — high scam/risk profile |
| Dispute over deposit | Photos of room at check-in/out; platform dispute within policy window |
| Long-stay falls through | Weekly-stay hotel apartment brands as bridge |

**Plan B brand mindset (examples, not endorsements):** major global chains + well-known Chinese business hotels that routinely scan foreign passports in Tier-1 cities.

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- First night = **boring reliable hotel**. Optimize price from night two.
- Keep hotel business card with **Chinese address** for taxi drivers (see [Emergency contacts](../../03-Emergency-DR/emergency-contacts-card/)).
- Screenshot host chat promising “foreigners OK” before you fly.
- For families: confirm **elevator / crib / extra bed** in writing, not only listing photos.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I stay at a friend’s home?**  
A: Friends often must help with registration at the local police station. Do not skip it.

**Q: Is Airbnb always OK for foreigners?**  
A: Platform varies by city and host compliance. Always verify registration path, not only the booking email.

**Q: What if police check the room?**  
A: Show passport + registration proof. Unregistered stays are the problem to avoid.
