**TL;DR:** Police **110** · Ambulance **120** · Fire **119** · NIA **12367**. Carry a **paper** hotel card with the Chinese address. Faster print: repo **`print-pack.html`**.

**Prerequisites:**
- Physical printout or laminated card (phone battery dies)
- Hotel front-desk card the moment you check in
- Passport for hospital / police procedures
- Embassy/consulate numbers verified on official sites before the trip

---

## Problem

In a real emergency your English, apps, and battery all degrade. A one-page card with numbers + Chinese address is the lowest-tech fix that still works.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Save the critical numbers

| Service | Number | Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Police** | 110 | Stay on the line. Say **“English, please.”** |
| **Ambulance** | 120 | State location; use Alipay/WeChat translation if needed |
| **Fire** | 119 | Active fire/rescue only |
| **NIA hotline** | 12367 | 24/7 visa/entry English support |

### 2. Pin embassy / consulate (Shanghai examples)

> [!WARNING]
> Numbers and addresses change. **Verify on official consulate sites before travel.**

| Country | Phone | Area (Shanghai) | Official site |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **USA** | 021-8011-2200 | Huaihai Middle Rd 1469 | [shanghai.usconsulate.gov](https://www.shanghai.usconsulate.gov) |
| **UK** | 021-3279-2000 | Nanjing West Rd 968 | [gov.uk/world/china](https://www.gov.uk/world/china) |
| **Canada** | 021-3279-2800 | Nanjing West Rd 1788 | [shanghai.gc.ca](https://www.shanghai.gc.ca) |
| **Australia** | 021-2215-5200 | Fuxing Middle Rd 1376 | [china.embassy.gov.au](https://china.embassy.gov.au) |
| **Germany** | 021-3401-0106 | Yongfu Rd 181 | [china.diplo.de](https://china.diplo.de) |

### 3. Build the physical card
1. Print [print-pack.html](../../../print-pack.html) **or** write: 110 / 120 / embassy / hotel Chinese address / your name + passport number.
2. Put one copy in wallet, one in each adult’s bag, one in stroller if traveling with kids.
3. **Star** consulate + hotel in your map app offline.

### 4. Survival phrases (say or show)

| Phrase | Chinese | Pinyin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Help! | 救命！ | Jiù mìng! |
| I'm lost. | 我迷路了。 | Wǒ mílù le. |
| I'm allergic. | 我过敏。 | Wǒ guòmǐn. |
| Call the police. | 我要报警。 | Wǒ yào bàojǐng. |
| Take me to… | 请带我去… | Qǐng dài wǒ qù… |

### 5. Verification
- [ ] You can dial 110/120 without unlocking obscure menus
- [ ] Hotel card is paper, not only a phone screenshot
- [ ] Family members each have a copy

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

### Phone dead and you need help
1. **5-star hotel lobby** (Marriott / Hyatt / etc.) — concierge is trained for foreigner emergencies and charging.
2. **Police kiosk / booth** near metro exits or major junctions.
3. **Show hotel card** to a passerby and say **Help** / **帮帮我** — people usually assist when distress is obvious.

### Battery critical (under 10%)
Treat as incident: stop non-essential apps → charge at café/hotel → do not start a long DiDi ride until above 20%.
