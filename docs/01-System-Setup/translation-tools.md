# 🗣️ Translation Tools & Language Barrier SOP

**TL;DR:** Download **Pleco** (dictionary) and **Google Translate** (offline) before arrival. For real-time conversation, use **WeChat Translate** or **Baidu Translate**. Keep a printed **emergency phrase card** for critical situations (hospital, police, lost).

**Prerequisites:**
- **Offline Dictionary:** Pleco (iOS/Android) - works without internet.
- **Translation App:** Google Translate or Baidu Translate (with offline Chinese pack).
- **WeChat:** Installed with translation features enabled.
- **Emergency Phrases:** Printed card with critical phrases in Chinese.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Pre-Installation (Before Arrival)
- **Step 1:** Download **Pleco** (free, with paid add-ons):
  - iOS: App Store
  - Android: Google Play
  - **Why:** Works 100% offline, includes handwriting recognition, and has medical/technical terms.
- **Step 2:** Download **Google Translate** and install the **Chinese (Simplified) offline pack**:
  - Open app → Settings → Offline Translation → Download Chinese
- **Step 3:** Enable **WeChat Translation**:
  - WeChat → Settings → General → Language → Enable Translation
- **Verification:** Test all three apps offline (turn off Wi-Fi/data) to ensure they work.

### 2. Real-Time Conversation Translation
- **Step 1:** For **spoken conversation**, use:
  - **Google Translate:** Tap microphone → Speak in English → Shows Chinese text + audio
  - **Baidu Translate:** Similar functionality, sometimes more accurate for Chinese
- **Step 2:** For **text translation** (menus, signs, documents):
  - **Google Translate Camera:** Point camera at text → Instant translation overlay
  - **Pleco OCR:** Take photo → Extract text → Translate
- **Verification:** Test with a simple phrase before relying on it in critical situations.

### 3. WeChat Translation (Built-in)
- **Step 1:** When someone sends you a Chinese message in WeChat:
  - Long-press the message → Select **"Translate"**
  - The message is instantly translated to English
- **Step 2:** To send a translated message:
  - Type in English → Long-press → Select **"Translate"** → Send the Chinese version
- **Verification:** WeChat translation works even without VPN (it's a Chinese app).

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

### If translation apps fail or you're in a critical situation:
1. **The "Emergency Phrase Card":** Keep a printed card with these phrases:
   - "Wǒ bù dǒng zhōngwén" (I don't understand Chinese)
   - "Qǐng bāngzhù wǒ" (Please help me)
   - "Wǒ xūyào yīshēng" (I need a doctor)
   - "Wǒ mí lù le" (I'm lost)
2. **Hotel Concierge:** International hotels have English-speaking staff. Ask them to write your destination or question in Chinese.
3. **Embassy Hotline:** Most embassies have 24/7 hotlines with translation services for emergencies.

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- **The "Offline First" Rule:** Always download offline packs before arrival. You may not have internet when you need translation most (underground, rural areas, VPN failures).
- **Pleco is Life:** Pleco is the gold standard for Chinese-English dictionaries. The free version is sufficient, but paid add-ons (medical dictionary, business terms) are worth it for long-term stays.
- **Handwriting Recognition:** Pleco's handwriting input is faster than typing Pinyin for unknown characters. Draw the character on screen → Get translation.
- **The "Photo Translation" Hack:** Use Google Translate's camera feature to translate entire menus, signs, or documents instantly. Works offline if you've downloaded the language pack.

## 📝 Essential Phrases Card (Print This)

### Emergency Situations
- **I need help:** Wǒ xūyào bāngzhù (我需要帮助)
- **Call the police:** Qǐng bǎng wǒ bàojǐng (请帮我报警)
- **I need a doctor:** Wǒ xūyào yīshēng (我需要医生)
- **I'm lost:** Wǒ mí lù le (我迷路了)
- **Where is the bathroom?** Cèsuǒ zài nǎlǐ? (厕所在哪里？)

### Transportation
- **Where is the metro station?** Dìtiě zhàn zài nǎlǐ? (地铁站在哪里？)
- **How much does it cost?** Duōshǎo qián? (多少钱？)
- **I want to go to [place]:** Wǒ xiǎng qù [dìdiǎn] (我想去[地点])

### Restaurants
- **I'm allergic to [food]:** Wǒ duì [shípǐn] guòmǐn (我对[食品]过敏)
- **No spicy, please:** Bùyào là (不要辣)
- **The bill, please:** Mǎidān (买单)

### Shopping
- **How much?** Duōshǎo qián? (多少钱？)
- **Too expensive:** Tài guì le (太贵了)
- **Do you accept credit card?** Kěyǐ shuākǎ ma? (可以刷卡吗？)

## 🚩 Strategic Gap: The "SPOF" Warning
**Single Point of Failure:** Relying on a single translation app or internet connection.
- **Hotfix:** Always maintain multiple translation redundancies: offline dictionary (Pleco), offline translator (Google Translate), and a printed phrase card. Never rely on internet-dependent apps alone.
