## Problem

Travelers bounce GZ ↔ SZ on short HSR hops and under-budget weather, luggage humidity, and “which airport/station” confusion.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Airports & stations (orientation)

| City | Common gateways (examples) | Runtime note |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Guangzhou** | CAN airport; Guangzhou South rail | South station is a long metro/taxi from old town — budget time |
| **Shenzhen** | SZX airport; Futian / North rail nodes | City is wide; pick hotel near the side you actually use |

**Action:** Match hotel district to **first meeting / first tourist node**, not “city name only.”

### First-night corridor (Guangzhou / Shenzhen)
- **Guangzhou:** Prefer hotels near **Tianhe / Zhujiang New Town** if flying into CAN and working downtown; match rail arrivals to **Guangzhou South** vs city stations.
- **Shenzhen:** Pick **Futian / Nanshan / Luohu** based on meetings or border plans — the city is wide.
- **Move:** Metro QR + DiDi; do not assume one hotel covers both GZ and SZ on the same night without a transfer plan.

### 2. Metro & pay

- Transit QR via Alipay/WeChat is the default tourist path (enable the correct city in-app).
- Expect bilingual signage in core lines; still keep [visual signs](../shanghai-visual-signs-guide/) habits.
- SZ and GZ are **not** one metro ticket universe — re-plan when you change cities.

### 3. Climate delta

- Hot, humid summers; sudden downpours.
- Pack light rain shell + phone waterproofing; electronics hate steam.
- Indoor malls are valid heat shelters (same as any Tier-1).

### 4. Hotels

National registration rules apply. Business districts (Tianhe, Futian, Nanshan class areas) have more foreigner-ready front desks. Small budget places: ask **可接待外宾吗** first ([Stay beyond hotel](../../01-System-Setup/stay-beyond-hotel/)).

### 5. Cross-border awareness (not a how-to)

Shenzhen sits next to Hong Kong. **Entry rules, visas, and e-channels differ by passport.**  

**Do:** Read your own visa/permit docs + official HK/mainland sources.  
**Don’t:** Assume 144-hour / visa-free tricks apply without checking **your** nationality and route.

### 6. First 24h checklist

- [ ] Correct airport/station on tickets  
- [ ] Hotel district matches tomorrow’s plan  
- [ ] Transit QR works  
- [ ] Cash + QR both tested  
- [ ] Rain plan if forecast storms

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

| Issue | Move |
|:---|:---|
| HSR sold out GZ–SZ | Full trains earlier/later; bus as last resort; avoid random drivers |
| Flooded streets / typhoon signal | Indoor day; delay intercity; follow local metro notices |
| Hotel refusal | CBD international chains |
| Heat exhaustion risk | Malls, metro AC, oral rehydration from convenience stores |
| Need English medical | International clinics in Tier-1 cores — see [Hospital access](../../03-Emergency-DR/hospital-access/) |
