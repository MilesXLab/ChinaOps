Landing gates (do not leave airport until ✓)

  1. ☐ Immigration / digital arrival card done
  2. ☐ Data works (open google.com or WhatsApp) — eSIM or airport Wi‑Fi
  3. ☐ Small test pay (Alipay or card) at FamilyMart / café
  4. ☐ Ride path ready (DiDi / Alipay transport or official taxi queue only)
  5. ☐ Hotel address screenshot + this paper card
  6. ☐ Battery safe (>20% or charging)

Money stack (second life after Alipay)

Layers
A) Alipay + WeChat QR
B) Physical Visa/Mastercard
C) Cash ¥500–1500
Prefer BOC / ICBC ATMs for foreign cards.
If pay fails
1. Switch Alipay ↔ WeChat
2. Other card in app
3. Chip card at terminal
4. Cash (break ¥100 at convenience store)

Network triage

SymptomDo this
Chinese sites work, Google does notVPN issue → switch server/protocol/app (need 2 VPNs)
Nothing loadsSIM/data → Airplane 10s → APN → backup eSIM → hotel Wi‑Fi
VPN connects then diesPort 443 / other protocol · swap Wi‑Fi ↔ mobile
Total offlineOffline maps · cash · paper hotel card · 5★ lobby help

Hard rules

  • Never follow hallway “Taxi? Cheap!” touts — official queue only.
  • Power banks: cabin only; domestic flights often need CCC + QR.
  • Hotels need physical passport for police registration (24h).
  • Do not casually log out of WeChat / swap SIM on payment phone.
