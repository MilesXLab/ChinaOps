# 💳 Lost or Stolen Bank Card Recovery SOP

<div class="plain-summary">
  <strong class="plain-summary-label">Plain English</strong>
  <p>Freeze the card in your bank app, then freeze linked Alipay/WeChat wallets if needed.</p>
  <p>Keep one backup payment path (second card or cash) before you travel.</p>
  <p><span class="scope-badge">Scope: national</span></p>
</div>

**TL;DR:** Immediately freeze your card via your bank's app or hotline. Use backup payment methods (Alipay/WeChat Pay balance, cash, or another card) while waiting for replacement. International banks may take 7-14 days to deliver a new card to China.

**Prerequisites:**
- **Bank App:** Mobile banking app installed on your phone.
- **Bank Hotline:** 24/7 customer service number (saved separately from your phone).
- **Backup Payment:** Alipay/WeChat Pay balance or another card.
- **Physical Cash:** At least 500-1000 RMB emergency cash.

---

## 📋 The Runbook

### 1. Immediate Card Freeze (The "Lockdown" Protocol)
- **Step 1:** If you have your phone, open your **bank's mobile app** and navigate to **Card Management** → **Freeze Card** (冻结卡片).
- **Step 2:** If no phone access, call your bank's 24/7 hotline:
  - **Visa/Mastercard:** Call the number on the back of your card (if you have it) or use the global hotline.
  - **US Banks:** Most have China-specific numbers (e.g., Chase: +86-400-120-8195).
  - **UK Banks:** HSBC China: +86-400-820-3098
- **Verification:** Confirm the card is frozen by attempting a small transaction (it should be declined).

### 2. Secure Payment Apps (If Card Was Linked)
- **Step 1:** If your lost card was linked to **Alipay** or **WeChat Pay**, remove it immediately:
  - Alipay: Me → Bank Cards → Select card → Remove
  - WeChat Pay: Me → Pay → Wallet → Cards → Remove
- **Step 2:** Add a backup card (if available) or use existing balance.
- **Verification:** Ensure payment apps still function with alternative funding sources.

### 3. Report to Bank (Formal Process)
- **Step 1:** Call your bank's customer service and report the card as **lost/stolen**.
- **Step 2:** Request a **replacement card**. Ask about delivery options:
  - **Express Delivery:** May cost extra (50-200 RMB) but arrives in 3-5 days.
  - **Standard Delivery:** Free but takes 7-14 days.
- **Verification:** Confirm the replacement card will be sent to your current address in China (hotel/residence).

---

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

### If you cannot reach your bank or need immediate cash:
1. **The "Wise/Revolut" Node:** If you have a Wise or Revolut account, use their app to withdraw cash from ATMs (up to daily limit, usually 1,000-2,000 RMB).
2. **Western Union:** Use Western Union to send money to yourself. You can pick it up at any China Post office with your passport.
3. **Embassy Emergency Loan:** Contact your embassy for a small emergency loan (usually 500-2,000 RMB, must be repaid).
4. **Family/Friends:** Use WeChat Pay or Alipay to receive money from family/friends (they can send via WeChat/Alipay even from abroad).

---

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- **The "Multi-Card" Strategy:** Always carry at least two cards from different banks. Keep one in your wallet, one in your hotel safe.
- **ATM Daily Limits:** Most international cards have daily withdrawal limits (usually 1,000-2,000 RMB). Plan accordingly if you need large amounts.
- **Card Replacement Address:** When requesting replacement, use your hotel address. Hotels are accustomed to receiving mail for guests. Provide the hotel's Chinese address and your room number.
- **The "Digital Card" Hack:** Some banks (like HSBC, Citibank) offer virtual cards in their apps. You can use these immediately while waiting for the physical replacement.

---

## 🔄 Recovery Protocol (After Freezing)

### 1. Alternative Payment Methods
- **Alipay/WeChat Balance:** Top up your balance using another card or receive money from family/friends.
- **Cash Withdrawal:** Use your backup card at ATMs (Bank of China, ICBC accept most international cards).
- **Hotel Credit:** Some hotels allow you to charge expenses to your room and pay at checkout.

### 2. Monitor Transactions
- **Step 1:** Check your bank statement daily for unauthorized transactions.
- **Step 2:** If you see fraudulent charges, report them immediately to your bank.
- **Verification:** Most banks have zero-liability policies for unauthorized transactions if reported within 48 hours.

### 3. Receive Replacement Card
- **Step 1:** When the replacement card arrives, activate it via your bank's app or hotline.
- **Step 2:** Update your payment apps with the new card details.
- **Verification:** Test with a small transaction to ensure the new card works.

---

## 🚩 Strategic Gap: The "SPOF" Warning
**Single Point of Failure:** Relying on a single payment method (one card, one payment app).
- **Hotfix:** Maintain multiple payment redundancies: two cards, Alipay balance, WeChat Pay balance, and physical cash. Never rely on a single payment node.
