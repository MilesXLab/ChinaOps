**TL;DR:** Tourist / visa-free entry ≠ work rights. Foreign-card Alipay ≠ full mainland banking. Do not stake rent, salary, or school on informal “workarounds.”

**Prerequisites:** [Visa & entry](../visa-and-entry/) · [Money Runtime](../money-runtime/) · [China App Stack](../china-app-stack/) · [Stay beyond hotel](../stay-beyond-hotel/)

## Problem

After 30–90 days, people hit walls: phone number expiry, inability to open local accounts, landlord demands, client payments, and immigration status grey zones. Forum tips age badly.

---

## 📋 The Runbook (boundary checks)

### 1. Status vs activity

| You may assume | Reality check |
|:---|:---|
| “I’m remote for a foreign company, so it’s fine.” | Host-country rules may still treat local work or certain activities as regulated — **verify for your nationality and visa type** |
| “Visa-free days are for anything.” | Entry purpose and permitted activities are not identical across categories |
| “I’ll just overstay a few days.” | Overstay is a hard risk (fines, exit trouble, future visas) — plan exit **before** day N |

**Action:** Keep a calendar of **must-exit / must-extend** dates. Set two reminders (7 days and 48 hours prior).

### 2. Banking boundaries

| Pattern | Risk | Safer pattern |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Rely only on one foreign card in Alipay | Mid-trip KYC / decline | Second card + cash buffer ([Money Runtime](../money-runtime/)) |
| Use someone else’s mainland account | Account freezes, disputes, compliance issues | Pay with instruments in **your** name |
| Expect easy local bank account as tourist | Many branches refuse short status | Plan without local CNY account unless advised by bank + your documents support it |
| Crypto / P2P grey cash-in | Scams + compliance | Stick to official card rails you understand |

### 3. Phone number & account lock-in

Long stays often burn a **tourist SIM**. When it dies:

- Alipay / WeChat / 12306 / delivery recovery can break together ([App stack](../china-app-stack/)).
- **Before** number expiry: add backup login paths, export critical bookings, keep passport KYC ready.
- Do not experiment with SIM swaps on the **primary WeChat device** mid-crisis.

### 4. Housing & registration

- Multi-month rentals need clear **who registers you with police** ([Stay beyond hotel](../stay-beyond-hotel/)).
- Informal sublets without registration are a dual risk (you + host).
- Deposits: document condition with photos; do not assume tourist platform rules apply to private landlords.

### 5. “Work permit / invitation” conversations

If anyone offers:

- Fake employment letters
- Borrowed company slots
- “Tourist visa but full-time local work”

Treat as **red flag**. Use licensed counsel / employer mobility teams — not Telegram fixers.

### 6. Monthly long-stay health check

- [ ] Status end date still ≥ 14 days away or extension path booked
- [ ] Two payment methods work
- [ ] SIM / eSIM expiry known
- [ ] Registration address matches where you sleep
- [ ] Critical docs offline (passport, insurance, lease)

---

## 🚨 Fallback (Plan B)

| Pressure | Move |
|:---|:---|
| Status days running out | Exit or lawful extension path — do not gamble |
| Primary card dead | Second card + cash; reduce spend; insurer/employer wire only via official channels |
| SIM dead, apps locked | Hotel Wi‑Fi + passport appeals; do not buy random “unban” services |
| Landlord dispute | Platform mediation if any; written evidence; consulate list is information not free lawyer |
| Job offer appears mid-trip | Pause start date until status is lawful for that work |

---

## What this page deliberately does **not** do

- Tell you which visa to apply for
- Promise a bank will open an account
- Validate digital-nomad marketing claims
- Replace an immigration lawyer or tax advisor

When money or status is material, **pay for professional advice** in your jurisdiction and China’s.

---

## 💡 TechDad's Tips

- Write your “day 0 / day 30 / day 60” money and SIM plan **before** week two feels comfortable.
- Keep an exit ticket flexible if status is short.
- Family long-stay: each adult has independent pay + registration story — not one shared grey account.
